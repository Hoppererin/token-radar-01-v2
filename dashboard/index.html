How the board is built

One snapshot in, one ranking out. No names are special-cased: the same curve prices every row, and the comparables come from this snapshot alone.

The curve

Each dot is one traded token in this snapshot, one per ticker. The line is the fitted curve. A flat multiple would be a line of slope 1.00 through the same dots — the market does not pay that: big protocols trade at a lower multiple than small ones, and the curve says by how much.

The blind test

The curve was not picked by taste. Three models were fitted on the traded tokens and tested on tokens they had not seen — first by repeated 5-fold cross-validation, then walk-forward: fitted only on protocols listed before a cutoff date, tested on the ones listed after it. The error is the median absolute log error on the held-out market caps; lower is better.

Who gets ranked

The board covers the snapshot's whole 40-protocol watchlist, then every other protocol that records no token and passes the watchlist's own exclusion rule without its $50M floor: no exchange, no bridge, no liquid staking or restaking, no versioned pool, no wrapped or staked product, no exchange-branded product. Protocols that record a token are set aside in the Have a token view.

What “records no token” means

DefiLlama's record is not proof: a protocol on this board may still have a token its record does not carry. The board ranks what the record shows and says so on every row — it never claims a protocol has no token, only that none is recorded.

What the snapshot does not carry

The snapshot carries no volume and no social figures, so no volume or social signal can be derived from it. The only momentum figure it carries is the 7-day TVL change, shown on the board; it was tested as an extra term and did not improve the blind test, so it does not move the value.
